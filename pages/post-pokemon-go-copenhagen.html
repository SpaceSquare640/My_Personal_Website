Copenhagen Top 5 — Pokémon GO Hotspot Coordinates

Five curated catch spots across the fairy-tale city — parks, harbor and beach — with coordinates, featured Pokémon and trainer tips.

Copenhagen is more than a fairy-tale city — for trainers it's a playground full of treasure. Whether you're planning a trip to Denmark or just looking for a fresh place to grind, these five curated hotspots will supercharge your Pokédex progress.

📍 Five Recommended Hotspots
01 🏟️

Fælledparken

55.701696, 12.56613 Google Maps →
Featured Pokémon Zeraora (Special Research), Mega Mewtwo (5-star raids), Paldean Tauros (Fighting), Comfey, event-cap Pikachu.
Why Go Vast open grounds with fresh air and multiple themed habitats — ideal for trainers who love mass catching in wide spaces and high-intensity raid sessions.
02 🎡

Tivoli Gardens

55.6736, 12.5681 Google Maps →
Featured Pokémon Poké Ball-cap Caterpie, Abra, Eevee, Pidgey.
Why Go Right in the city centre with extremely dense PokéStops — a sanctuary for stop-spinning and item resupply. Your bag will almost never run dry here!
03 ⚓

Nyhavn

55.6805, 12.5901 Google Maps →
Featured Pokémon Squirtle, Psyduck, Shellder, Primal Kyogre.
Why Go A picture-perfect waterfront district — ideal for focusing on Water-type Pokémon. Fill your Pokédex while admiring the colourful townhouses.
04 🏰

Kongens Have

55.6844, 12.5744 Google Maps →
Featured Pokémon Comfey, Scyther, Butterfree, Paras, Mudbray, Charcadet, Dhelmise.
Why Go Copenhagen's oldest park and the traditional gathering ground of the local player community. If you want to experience the local trainer vibe, this is the place.
05 🏖️

Amager Beach Park

55.6580, 12.6290 Google Maps →
Featured Pokémon Krabby, Staryu, Corsola, Vulpix (rainy-weather special: Poliwag).
Why Go A superb coastal position — perfect when you're chasing a specific weather boost or need to mass-catch Water-types to clear tasks.
💡 Trainer Tips
🌦️
Watch the Weather
Copenhagen weather changes fast. Check the in-game weather icon and pick the spot that matches the boost you're after.
🔁
Events Rotate Spawns
Spawn pools shift with official Seasons and events. If a spot's Pokémon don't match this list, that's normal — check the current event announcements.
🎒
Bag Management
Tivoli Gardens has a huge number of PokéStops — clear your bag before you go so no precious items go to waste.
